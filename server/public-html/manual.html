How to use the C-Mail E-Mail Manager

Getting Started
Step 1: Download

Currently we only support Windows. You can download the executeable playing our little seeking game, where you have to find the download link within dozens of ads. Be assured that our software will not contain any advertisment or paywall. After the download, simply start the application.

Step 2: Login

You will be asked to login with your email account. Since we work with IMAP you will have to enable it at your provider. If you need more information how to that, you will find links to the instructions below.

  • GMAIL- Gmail Accounts with 2FA need to use a App-Password to login
  • GMX
  • OUTLOOK/HOTMAIL

To log in, simply put in your e-mail and (depending on your provider) either your normal e-mail password or an app password (e.g. for gmail). You can press the eye to make change the visibility of your password.

Depending on the amount of emails your account holds, you might need to wait a momment, while the system is loading and sorting. The loading screen will keep you up to date about the progress.

Step 3: Manage your Emails

The main windows gives you an overiew over your received emails, grouped by the sender. If it was recognised as newletter you will get the option to unsubscribe. Generally you are able to delete all emails from a sender with the delete button.


You can also order your mails by left-clicking on the field you want to order by. Each field can be sorted ascending or descending:


If you want to just see the mail addresses that have an unsubscribe link or filter for a specific sender, you can do so by clicking the checkbox next to the Newsletter option or write the name of the sender in the input field and press the filter button to apply the filters. Should you want to get back to the unfiltered view, simply delete your input from the field, uncheck the box again and press the filter button once again.



To unsubscribe from a newsletter, left click on the unsubscribe button in the corresponding row. It will open a dialog, in which you will have to choose the option "Unsubscribe":

To delete ALL emails from a sender, select the trash-can symbol and press continue in the opened dialog:

Step 4: Logout

To log out, just close the application through clicking the close button in the top right corner or use Alt+F4.

FAQ
"I can not log in"

There is clear feedback given if your credentials are missing or invalid.
Please double check if you have IMAP enabled and in case you are using GMAIL, you have to use the App-Password.

"I deleted something important"

There has been a confirmation. We are not able to restore emails!

"Is my data safe?"

Yes your data is safe. We do not keep any data that you input. All the processing happens on the server side of your email provider.

"I cant find my provider in the list of supported mail providers. Can I still use your application?"

No, you can only use mails from the supported providers with this application. There will be updates with added functionality for more providers soon though!
